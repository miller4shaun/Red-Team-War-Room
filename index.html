P7 review: what we decided vs. what happened

Our P7 plan, what the team actually entered (read from MEGA's P8 screens, where the sliders still show our P7 choices), and how it turned out.

What worked

  • ✓Image made $1,755, up from a $65 loss. The higher price and leaner spending paid off in one period.
  • ✓HR & Quality worked. Keeping it at $1,040 cut our cost of failures in half ($534 → $266). Failure rates fell 3 points in every market.
  • ✓Eco design appeal nearly doubled (56% → 102%) after we raised engineering, as planned.
  • ✓Best retained earnings in the universe, and profit 2.6× P6.

What it cost us

  • ✕Family marketing cut ($1,118 → $644) halved orders (95 → 48). We saved $474 and lost about $2,800 of revenue. Family lost $678, and the plant ran at 19%.
  • ✕Low Cost: built 56 of 63 possible, turned away 14 buyers. Low Cost buyers punish stock-outs, so some won't come back in P8.
  • ✕Eco: grew the plant to 150 and built at full production. 50 cars are now unsold, and the plant is about twice the size of our Eco market.
  • ✕Image design appeal fell (191% → 106%) after engineering was cut from $942 to $522. Image buyers care most about engineering, so this is the biggest risk for P8.
  • ✕Image at $128 while Blue charged $140 and Green $142. We still sold out, so the extra $7–14 a car was free money left on the table.

Coach's rules for the two-region game

From the professor's "Explore More: Merger" video walkthrough.

  • 1Forecast from your own sales history. Check units sold last period, the period before, and the one before that, then extend that running trend forward.
  • 2Each region has its own indices (business and production cost). Region 2 is not growing or shrinking at Region 1's rate, so forecast each separately.
  • 3The 9 engineering sliders are shared. Moving Low Cost or Family engineering in one region moves it in the other. There is one car design for both plants.
  • 4Marketing sliders are separate per region. Set Region 2 marketing on its own.
  • 5Never set sliders to zero to stop building. A zero there zeroes the car in both regions. Control how many cars you build with the production number on the Budget page.
  • 6Budget steps, every time: enter market size, check inventory, check how many you can build, then work out the market share needed to sell them.
  • 7Eco and Image are unknown in Region 2. Breaking in needs heavy marketing per car for the first few periods. Don't ship a pile of inventory there expecting it to sell in year one.
  • 8Read the after-merger cheat sheet (in MEGA under Documents), especially the import/export transfer-pricing diagram.

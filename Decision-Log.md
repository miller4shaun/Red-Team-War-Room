## P4: Starting point (baseline)
- **Did:** Every team started with the same company: Region 1, all four car lines. Prices were $40 Low Cost, $60 Family, $65 Eco and $120 Image.
- **What happened:** Net profit −$302 for everyone.
- **Lesson:** Everyone starts equal, so every gap after this comes from decisions.

## P5: First moves (mixed)
- **Did:** Low Cost price to $42 and Eco to $69. Pushed Image design to 165% appeal. Raised marketing on Low Cost (280 → 490), Eco (650 → 1,211) and Image (→ 1,932).
- **Why:** Lead on Image design and charge more for Eco.
- **What happened:** Net profit −$506. Image had 88 buyers but only 67 cars. Low Cost had 64 buyers but only 51 cars.
- ✓ Image design drew buyers (88 orders vs 60).
- ✕ Ran out of cars: lost 21 Image and 13 Low Cost sales.
- ✕ Eco design fell to 63% while Purple reached 189% and Yellow 134%.
- **Lesson:** Demand we create is wasted if we don't have the cars.

## P6: First profit (worked)
- **Did:** Image design to 191%, price $126. Raised HR & Quality spending sharply (about $150 → $1,040). Cut Eco marketing (1,211 → 394) and Eco price to $63.
- **Why:** Lower failure and quality costs, and keep the Image lead.
- **What happened:** Net profit +$394, our first profit. Image sold 85 to 97 buyers. Family overbuilt and had 42 left over.
- ✓ Image is the money maker: $10.7k revenue.
- ✓ The higher HR & Quality spending paid for itself.
- ✕ Still short on Image (12 lost sales).
- ✕ Family overbuilt.
- **Lesson:** Match production to buyers, in both directions.

## P7: Merger, Region 2 added (mixed)
- **Did:** Took on Region 2 (Low Cost and Family plants). Grew the Eco plant to 150. Family Dealer Proximity was cut 7 → 4 and Image Number of Models 7 → 4. Neither cut was in our plan.
- **Why:** Save money and build Eco capacity early.
- **What happened:**
  - Region 1 net profit +$1,029 (best yet) and Region 2 +$335.
  - Family buyers halved (95 → 48). The plant ran at 19% and Family lost $678.
  - Image design appeal fell from 191% to 106%.
- ✓ Image still earned +$1,755.
- ✓ Failure rates dropped.
- ✓ Region 2 Low Cost sold all 135 cars.
- ✕ The slider entry mistakes cost us half our Family buyers.
- ✕ The Eco plant was half empty: 48 sold, 50 left over.
- **Lesson:** Check every slider in MEGA against the plan before submitting.

## P8: The big bet, selling 3 plants (mixed)
- **Did:**
  - Sold both Low Cost plants and the Region 1 Family plant.
  - Eco design blitz (102% → 112%) at $62.
  - Image at $137 with design back up to 159%.
  - Region 2 Family: marketing up (648 → 944), price $58, plant grown to 221.
- **Why:** Leave crowded markets. Go all in where rivals are weak: Eco in Region 1 and Family in Region 2.
- **What happened:**
  - Net profit Region 1 −$2,394 and Region 2 −$759, from the plant-sale charges as planned.
  - **Blue, Yellow and Green all sold their Eco plants.**
  - Eco had **111 buyers but only 74 cars**.
  - Low Cost sold just 72, leaving **141 stuck**.
- ✓ Image sold 91 of 91 and earned +$1,764.
- ✓ Region 2 Family sold 78, the most in Region 2.
- ✓ We read the Eco exit right.
- ✕ The Eco plant ran at 17%, losing 37 sales (about $2,300).
- ✕ Low Cost sliders and marketing were cut while we still had cars to sell.
- ✕ The Family slider increases weren't entered, and Region 2 Family facility went in at $6,000 instead of the planned $552.
- **Lesson:** Keep supporting a line until its last car is sold. When we lead a market, build to full capacity.

## P9: Own Eco, fill Family (planned, waiting for results)
- **Did (plan):**
  - Eco at $69, build all 163, plant to 210.
  - Region 2 Family: build 115, all sold in Region 2. Consumption 4 → 6, Dealer Proximity 7 → 8, price $56.
  - Region 2 Family facility investment $552 (our call: keeps the plant near 221; the plant is only about 56% used, so we expect no benefit this period).
  - Region 1 Family: out of the market. No transfer of cars from Region 2 (our decision), marketing set to 0.
  - Image: Novelty 7 → 8, hold $137, build 80, no new capacity.
  - Low Cost (both plants already sold in P8): sell the 141 leftover cars at $37 (Region 1) and $28 (Region 2). Engineering sliders go above the market, which is free with no plant. Dealer marketing goes up (Region 2 proximity 4).
  - Not entering Region 2 Eco or Image: that needs a new facility investment, and our cash goes to our two lead markets.
  - Repay $4,000 of Region 1 debt.
- **Why:**
  - Eco buyers are flat at about 265, and only Purple is left. Buyers chose us 111 to 41 over Purple in P8.
  - Region 2 Family buyers grew 13%, and rivals have only about 280 cars for about 355 buyers. We chose not to ship 60 cars to Region 1: that market is crowded (about 375 buyers for 434 rival cars) and the margin was thin (about $17 a car before marketing).
  - Image now has about 540 cars for about 460 buyers. Low Cost is flooded: about 590 cars for 340 buyers in Region 1, and about 1,380 for 620 in Region 2.
  - Gas index 2.4 rewards fuel economy. Region 2 builds are 27% cheaper. Our Region 2 CO2 rank is last.
- **Predicted rivals:**
  - Blue: Image toward $145.
  - Green: opens Region 2 Image and grows Region 2 Family.
  - Yellow: dumps stuck cars.
  - Purple: Eco price cuts, and may sell plants.
- **Expected:** EBIT about +$5.1k (Region 1) and +$0.5k (Region 2), after dropping the 60-car transfer. Eco sells out, Low Cost is cleared, and we move from #3 toward #1 on total profit ($540 behind Green).
- **What happened:**
  - Net profit Region 1 +$2,181 and Region 2 −$1,656 (total +$525, against about +$5.6k EBIT expected). #2 on P9 profit and #2 on cumulative P4–P9 (+$4,282 for Blue, −$765 for us). Blue is about $5k ahead.
  - Eco: 104 sold at $69, EBIT +$1,260, but **60 cars left unsold** (plan assumed a full sell-out).
  - Image: 91 sold at $137, EBIT +$1,105. Blue sold 127 at $150 and earned +$3,790 in Region 1.
  - Region 2 Family: 117 sold at $56, the most in Region 2, but EBIT −$1,166. Marketing was $1,671 against $1,727 gross profit. Blue spent $929 to sell 75.
  - Region 2 Low Cost: 352 orders, only 113 sold (239 lost, no plant), EBIT −$356. Blue's Region 2 Low Cost plant ran at 95% use and earned +$1,508 at a $18.6 unit cost.
  - Region 2 HR & Quality jumped from $597 to $1,600, which was not in the plan.
  - Debt: repaid $4,000 of Region 1 debt as planned.
- ✓ Eco is our best line and we own the market.
- ✓ Region 2 Family sold out and led the region on volume.
- ✓ Region 2 Low Cost stock was cleared, and Region 1 swung back to a profit.
- ✕ Region 2 Family marketing ate the whole margin.
- ✕ Selling the Low Cost plants in P8 gave up Blue's most profitable non-Image line.
- ✕ Eco overbuilt by 60 cars, and the Region 2 HR jump was unplanned.
- **Rivals scored:** Blue Image toward $145: right direction, Blue went to $150 ✓. Green opens Region 2 Image: Green had 107 Image orders in Region 2 ✓ (Family growth not checked). Yellow dumps stuck cars: Yellow still had 145 Family cars in Region 1 ✕. Purple Eco price cuts: not checked.
- **Lesson:** Winning on volume isn't winning on profit. Region 2 Family led the region but lost money because marketing outgrew the margin. Build to buyers, not to capacity: Eco's plant was full but the buyers weren't there. Check HR and marketing sliders against the plan, as in P7 and P8.

## P10 and P11: Plan to catch Blue (recession expected, size and timing unknown)
- **Goal:** Win on every line except Image and move past Blue on the overall leaderboard. We don't yet know the leaderboard metric.
- **Gap:** Blue is about $5k ahead on cumulative profit and earns about +$5.7k EBIT a period. Blue's Image is about two-thirds of that, so a recession that hits premium cars would help us.
- **Ideas agreed so far:**
  - **Eco:** Build fewer cars (60 are left over) and put more into marketing.
  - **Region 2 Family:** Keep the line and cut marketing toward Blue's level. Capacity isn't the limit (plant about 52% used), so more plant won't add sales.
  - **Region 2 Low Cost:** Consider rebuilding capacity if the payback works in two periods.
- **Open:** Leaderboard metric, plant cost and payback, and the recession's size and timing.

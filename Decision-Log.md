## P4: Starting point (baseline)
- **Did:** Every team started with the same company: Region 1, all four car lines. Prices were $40 Low Cost, $60 Family, $65 Eco and $120 Image.
- **What happened:** Net profit −$302 for everyone.
- **Lesson:** Everyone starts equal, so every gap after this comes from decisions.

## P5: First moves (mixed)
- **Did:** Low Cost price to $42 and Eco to $69. Pushed Image design to 165% appeal. Raised marketing on Low Cost (280 → 490), Eco (650 → 1,211) and Image (→ 1,932).
- **Why:** Lead on Image design and charge more for Eco.
- **What happened:** Net profit −$506. Image had 88 buyers but only 67 cars. Low Cost had 64 buyers but only 51 cars.
- ✓ Image design drew buyers (88 orders vs 60).
- ✕ Ran out of cars: lost 21 Image and 13 Low Cost sales.
- ✕ Eco design fell to 63% while Purple reached 189% and Yellow 134%.
- **Lesson:** Demand we create is wasted if we don't have the cars.

## P6: First profit (worked)
- **Did:** Image design to 191%, price $126. Raised HR & Quality spending sharply (about $150 → $1,040). Cut Eco marketing (1,211 → 394) and Eco price to $63.
- **Why:** Lower failure and quality costs, and keep the Image lead.
- **What happened:** Net profit +$394, our first profit. Image sold 85 to 97 buyers. Family overbuilt and had 42 left over.
- ✓ Image is the money maker: $10.7k revenue.
- ✓ The higher HR & Quality spending paid for itself.
- ✕ Still short on Image (12 lost sales).
- ✕ Family overbuilt.
- **Lesson:** Match production to buyers, in both directions.

## P7: Merger, Region 2 added (mixed)
- **Did:** Took on Region 2 (Low Cost and Family plants). Grew the Eco plant to 150. Family Dealer Proximity was cut 7 → 4 and Image Number of Models 7 → 4. Neither cut was in our plan.
- **Why:** Save money and build Eco capacity early.
- **What happened:**
  - Region 1 net profit +$1,029 (best yet) and Region 2 +$335.
  - Family buyers halved (95 → 48). The plant ran at 19% and Family lost $678.
  - Image design appeal fell from 191% to 106%.
- ✓ Image still earned +$1,755.
- ✓ Failure rates dropped.
- ✓ Region 2 Low Cost sold all 135 cars.
- ✕ The slider entry mistakes cost us half our Family buyers.
- ✕ The Eco plant was half empty: 48 sold, 50 left over.
- **Lesson:** Check every slider in MEGA against the plan before submitting.

## P8: The big bet, selling 3 plants (mixed)
- **Did:**
  - Sold both Low Cost plants and the Region 1 Family plant.
  - Eco design blitz (102% → 112%) at $62.
  - Image at $137 with design back up to 159%.
  - Region 2 Family: marketing up (648 → 944), price $58, plant grown to 221.
- **Why:** Leave crowded markets. Go all in where rivals are weak: Eco in Region 1 and Family in Region 2.
- **What happened:**
  - Net profit Region 1 −$2,394 and Region 2 −$759, from the plant-sale charges as planned.
  - **Blue, Yellow and Green all sold their Eco plants.**
  - Eco had **111 buyers but only 74 cars**.
  - Low Cost sold just 72, leaving **141 stuck**.
- ✓ Image sold 91 of 91 and earned +$1,764.
- ✓ Region 2 Family sold 78, the most in Region 2.
- ✓ We read the Eco exit right.
- ✕ The Eco plant ran at 17%, losing 37 sales (about $2,300).
- ✕ Low Cost sliders and marketing were cut while we still had cars to sell.
- ✕ The Family slider increases weren't entered, and Region 2 Family facility went in at $6,000 instead of the planned $552.
- **Lesson:** Keep supporting a line until its last car is sold. When we lead a market, build to full capacity.
